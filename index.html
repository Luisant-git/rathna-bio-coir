WHY US?

Benefits Of Cocopeat

Sustainability

Because coconut husks are plentiful, using them to make cocopeat promotes the coconut industry and reduces waste.

Water Retention

Because of its exceptional capacity to retain moisture for lengthy periods of time, cocopeat is perfect for gardening.

Biodegradable

Cocopeat promotes the nutrient cycle and is biodegradable, making it suitable for repeated use as a growing medium.

WHY ARE WE SUPERIOR?

Application Of Cocopeat

Commercial farms and nurseries utilize a lot of cocopeat to ensure a good crop. Perfect for mushroom farming, microgreens, potting mixes, and seed germination.

Plant growth benefits from cocopeat. It can be used as a soil conditioner, start seeds, mix pots, and enhance soil structure for plant growth.

Horticulture

Cocopeat is an inert substrate used in hydroponic systems that helps plants grow while holding on to moisture and nutrients.

Hydroponics

To assist maintain soil moisture, control soil temperature, and inhibit the growth of weeds, apply a layer of cocopeat mulch

Mulching

In order to improve soil structure, stabilize sloped land, stop soil erosion and to grow plant, cocopeat can be applied to the soil.
